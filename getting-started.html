Your guide to LeonidaTracker

A practical way to plan and record your GTA VI journey.

Updated 19 November 2026. LeonidaTracker is designed as a second screen for players. It does not connect to your console or read your GTA VI save file.

Start your own checklist

Open My journey, name a place, vehicle, mission or activity, and add a short note. Tick it off when you finish. You can search, filter, edit and remove your entries. The dashboard counts your personal entries separately from the old sample checklist.

Your list is private to the browser you use. It will not automatically appear on another phone or computer.

Explore the map with accurate expectations

The interactive illustration on the home page is an original fictional concept map. Its region pins are illustrative, not exact in-game coordinates. The six region names—Vice City, Leonida Keys, Grassrivers, Port Gellhorn, Ambrosia and Mount Kalaga—were introduced in Rockstar’s official GTA VI material. Use the map to browse region summaries; use My journey to record places you actually find.

Keep your progress when changing phones

  1. Open Save & install and select Download progress backup.
  2. Keep the JSON file in a safe location you control.
  3. On your other device, open LeonidaTracker and choose Restore from backup.

The restore merges your entries and completed checkmarks with those already on that device. Backups from Version 8 also work; they do not contain personal entries because that feature was added in Version 9.

Install it on Android

Open the site in Chrome, tap the browser menu, then choose Install app or Add to Home screen. The companion opens in its own window. Keep a backup before clearing browser data or changing devices.

What is confirmed?

Rockstar lists GTA VI for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S and dates its launch to 19 November 2026. Its official site is the source for story, people, places and announcements. LeonidaTracker labels sample tasks and the concept map clearly, and will add verified game locations only when checked against reliable sources. See Rockstar’s GTA VI page and Newswire for current information.
