Quick answers

Does this site track my in-game progress automatically?

No. Add and complete your own entries as you play. The sample checklist is separate from your personal journey.

Is this Rockstar’s official map?

No. The main interactive map is an attributed community reconstruction with approximate pins. Open Rockstar’s official GTA VI website for its printable map. The earlier concept map remains in the legacy section.

Will my entries transfer to another phone?

They stay in this browser until you export a backup and restore it on another device. Keep the backup file somewhere you can find it.
