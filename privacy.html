Privacy & saved progress

A clear look at what stays on your device.

Updated 19 November 2026.

Progress and personal notes

LeonidaTracker stores your checklist progress, bookmarks and personal entries in your browser’s local storage on your device. The site does not require an account and does not send this information to a LeonidaTracker server. Clearing browser data can remove it.

Backup files

When you download a backup, your browser creates a JSON file containing your entries and progress. You choose where to keep it and whether to import it on another device. The import reads the file in your browser and merges it into local storage.

Offline use and external links

A service worker caches site files for offline use. The site links to Rockstar Games and other external pages; their own privacy practices apply when you visit them.

Advertising and analytics

As of this update, LeonidaTracker does not run advertising or analytics scripts. If that changes, this page will be updated before those services are added.
